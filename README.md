<h1 align="center">Back in Stock for Craft CMS</h1>

Back in Stock is a Craft CMS plugin that turns sold-out Craft Commerce variants into a reason for customers to return. Collect interest at the product, notify the right people when inventory recovers, and keep the message on brand.

Add a notification form to a product page and subscribe guests or signed-in customers to the exact variant they want. Requests remain visible in Craft, giving the team a clear view of demand while the item is unavailable.

## Features

- Record the exact size, colour, or other variant a customer is waiting for.
- Accept notification requests from visitors with or without a Craft account.
- Control confirmation and availability messages with your own Twig templates.
- Decide how much inventory must be available and whether a variant must also be available for purchase before notifications are sent.
- Review notification requests and their status from the control panel.
- Remove completed requests automatically when your privacy policy calls for it.
