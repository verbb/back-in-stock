<!-- feature-intro -->
Back in Stock turns sold-out Craft Commerce variants into a reason for customers to return. Collect demand at the exact variant, notify the right customers when stock returns, and follow every delivery from one focused Craft control panel.
<!-- feature-intro-end -->

<!-- feature-section media-size="large" -->
## Capture exact demand

Add an unstyled notification form wherever a sold-out variant appears. Guests and signed-in customers can join the list for the precise size, colour, or configuration they want, while duplicate-safe registration keeps repeated submissions from multiplying future sends.

![An exact variant's active subscribers, with email search and state filtering.](../screenshots/product-subscriptions.png)
<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-media -->
## Find the product or customer you need

Search by product, variant, SKU, or subscriber email without wading through the full Commerce catalogue. Product type filters and sortable columns make it easy to move from a store-wide question to the exact variant behind it.

![Products with Back in Stock data, including exact variants, product types, SKUs and waiting counts.](../screenshots/products.png)
<!-- feature-media-end -->

<!-- feature-section media-size="large" -->
## Match attention to inventory

Notify the oldest subscribers in proportion to available units, tune how many people each unit should reach, or notify everyone who is waiting. Every restock starts one durable cycle, so repeated Commerce events cannot select the same subscriber twice while the variant remains available.

![A notification showing selected recipients, delivery outcomes and diagnostic details.](../screenshots/notification-detail.png)
<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-grid -->
- :icon[mail-check] **Verified interest** Optional double opt-in keeps only confirmed recipients active.
- :icon[layers-subtract] **Controlled batches** Queue selected notifications in predictable groups with bounded retries.
- :icon[message-cog] **Editable email** Use Craft System Messages or take over the full markup with Twig.
- :icon[link-off] **One-click cancellation** Signed links let recipients leave without an account.
- :icon[history] **Durable history** Trace each restock run and its sent, failed, cancelled, or uncertain outcomes.
- :icon[refresh] **Reconciliation** Recover missed stock signals and apply lifecycle retention on schedule.
<!-- feature-grid-end -->

<!-- feature-section media-size="large" -->
## Operate from one clear place

See current demand and recent activity in Overview, then use Products to find a variant by product title, SKU, or subscriber email. The list only contains variants with Back in Stock subscriptions or notification history, rather than the whole Commerce catalogue. Open a variant to see its current and past subscriptions alongside its notification activity.

![Overview showing the products customers are waiting for and recent lifecycle activity.](../screenshots/overview.png)
<!-- feature-section-end -->

## Follow the full lifecycle

See customers subscribe, verify, cancel, expire, and receive notifications in one chronological view for each variant. Notification entries lead to the delivery evidence behind the result, while everyday subscription events stay readable at a glance.

![Chronological subscription and notification activity for an exact product variant.](../screenshots/product-activity.png)
