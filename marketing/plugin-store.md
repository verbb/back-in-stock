Back in Stock turns sold-out Craft Commerce variants into a reason for customers to return. Collect interest at the product, notify the right people when inventory recovers, and keep the message on brand.

Add a notification form to a product page and subscribe guests or signed-in customers to the exact variant they want. Requests remain visible in Craft, giving the team a clear view of demand while the item is unavailable.

## Features

- **Variant-level interest:** Record the exact size, colour, or other variant a customer is waiting for.
- **Guests and customers:** Accept notification requests from visitors with or without a Craft account.
- **Custom email templates:** Control confirmation and availability messages with your own Twig templates.
- **Stock thresholds:** Decide how much inventory must be available and whether a variant must also be available for purchase before notifications are sent.
- **Request logs:** Review notification requests and their status from the control panel.
- **Optional purging:** Remove completed requests automatically when your privacy policy calls for it.
- **Restock notifications:** Choose the stock threshold that counts as available and let Craft’s queue deliver notifications when a subscribed variant crosses it. Custom subjects and Twig templates keep confirmation and availability emails consistent with your store.
